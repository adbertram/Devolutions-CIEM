# Account lifecycle policy

**Source URL:** https://docs.devolutions.net/pam/concepts/account-lifecycle-policy/

---

An
account lifecycle policy
enforces strict management rules for
privileged accounts
, particularly those at high risk of compromise. A key practice in such policies is rotating the account password after each use, ensuring credentials are not reused or left exposed for long periods.
In Devolutions products, this concept is implemented through features such as
password rotation
policies, heartbeat synchronization to validate credential accuracy, and automatic password
propagation
to all relevant systems. These combined tools help protect critical accounts by automating credential hygiene and reducing manual intervention.
This approach is essential for securing sensitive assets, especially when accounts are used in shared or automated environments. It also supports
compliance
with security best practices and frameworks that emphasize
just-in-time access
and minimal exposure of privileged credentials.
